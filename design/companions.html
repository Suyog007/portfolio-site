Pick a companion

Four original SVG characters with the same small engine behind them. Their heads and eyes follow your pointer with easing. They blink now and then, perk up when you hover a link, and doze off after about ten seconds of stillness. Move the mouse to wake them.

Robot my pick

A friendly little bot whose face is a screen. Its glowing eyes slide across the screen to follow you, and its antenna blinks when you hover a link. It matches the AI-agent and systems work you do without being on the nose. In dark mode it reads like a tiny terminal.

Dog

The original idea, redrawn as a white pup with floppy dark ears. It sits still and watches. Its ears lag a little behind its head so the motion feels soft, and when you hover a link its tail wags and its tongue pops out. Warm and familiar.

Red panda

A Himalayan animal, so it's a quiet nod to Nepal and gives the site a sense of place. It's the most colourful of the four, so I'd keep it small. Its ears perk up when you hover a link.

Owl

The night-owl developer. Owls really do turn their heads, so this one rotates the most. Its pupils widen when you hover a link. Bookish, which suits a writing-first site.

How it would sit on the real site

  • Size and spot: about 64–72px, tucked into the header next to your name on desktop. It never floats over content or blocks clicks.
  • Performance: no libraries. It only redraws while you're moving; otherwise the animation loop stops.
  • Touch: tap anywhere and it looks there. It's hidden on very small screens if it gets crowded.
  • Reduced motion: eyes still follow your pointer, but there's no easing, blinking, wagging or floating "z".
  • Optional extra: double-click it and it walks along the bottom edge for a moment, a small nod to the oneko-style chaser.
